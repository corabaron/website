I’m so glad you are interested in becoming a Research Assistant (RA)! The Research Assistant role is an opportunity to share your unique perspectives on psychology and how the field might move forward. RAs are vital collaborators in my research, offering insight into psychological mechanisms, proposing novel approaches to measuring variables, and working hands-on with research participants.

Open positions?

I am possibly recruiting new RAs for Fall quarter, 2026. Please reach out directly to me via email (corabaron@ucsb.edu) with your CV/resume and some information about you and your research interests. Recruited RAs will be assisting with data collection.
