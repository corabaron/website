Non-academic observations

Photography

Photography is inherently relational: it connects us to others through communicating shared memories, emotions, and moments of beauty.

Beyond the classroom and lab, photography has become a meaningful creative practice for me. My love for taking photos began somewhere between playing with my mom’s digital camera in the early 2000s and a high school photography class, and has grown ever since. More recently, I’ve been exploring film photography alongside digital work. Here’s a collection of favorites from over the years.

Tokyo, Japan

Japan stands apart from every place I’ve ever visited. Tokyo won me over completely with its wealth of shops devoted to cameras, film, and all things creative.

March 2026 · Fujifilm X-T30 II

Paris, France

Paris is one of the most special cities that I’ve ever visited. Documenting my solo trip there on film felt like the only fitting choice.

September 2025 · Olympus Trip AF 51 · Kodak Gold 200

Amsterdam, The Netherlands

I booked a very last-minute trip to Amsterdam with one goal in mind: to see the tulips. Flowers are one of my favorite subjects.

March 2025 · Canon G7X Mark II
